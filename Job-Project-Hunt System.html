01

Your positioning

Sell production capacity, not just freelance hours.

“I help agencies handle their WordPress production workload.”
Position yourself as a Senior WordPress Developer / Remote WordPress Production Developer—an experienced extension of an agency's team.

What you can handle

Figma → WordPressDiviElementorWooCommerceACFHTML/CSS/JSResponsive fixesCustom functionalityIntegrationsMaintenanceBug fixingSpeed / SEOMigrationsContent updates

Your value proposition

The agency's designers can design. Account managers can manage clients. You build, fix and maintain the websites.

Lead with experience, reliability, availability and production support. Discuss your $15/hour starting rate when relevant—don't make price your whole pitch.

02

Find your ideal agency

Look for teams likely to need recurring production support.

Best-fit profile

  • About 10–30 employees
  • WordPress-heavy portfolio
  • Divi, Elementor or WooCommerce projects
  • 20–50+ websites/projects
  • Active clients and maintenance work
  • Designers, account managers and PMs

Target markets

  • Australia first
  • New Zealand second
  • US, UK and Canada next
  • Web design/development agencies

Skip / deprioritize

  • Inactive agencies
  • No WordPress work
  • Mostly branding or social media
  • Solo freelancers with little recurring work
  • Very large enterprise teams

Where to find agencies

Google: “WordPress agency Melbourne”, “Elementor agency Australia”, “WooCommerce agency Sydney”, “web design agency Brisbane”.

LinkedIn: check employee count, services, portfolio and decision-makers.

Directories: Clutch, DesignRush, Sortlist and GoodFirms. Review agency websites directly, too.

03

Qualify each agency quickly

Spend around 2–4 minutes checking each prospect.

Quick checklist

Who to contact

First: Founder, owner, managing director.

Second: Technical director, head of development, development manager.

Third: Project manager, operations manager, digital director.

Start with one relevant person per agency.

04

Cold outreach

Short, specific and helpful beats a long sales pitch.

Copy-and-edit email

Subject: Extra WordPress development support for [Agency Name] Hi [Name], I came across [Agency Name] and noticed you work with quite a few WordPress websites. I'm a senior WordPress developer based in the Philippines, specializing in Divi, Elementor, WooCommerce, ACF and custom HTML/CSS/JS. I help agencies with the development/production side of their projects — turning Figma designs into WordPress, handling responsive fixes, custom functionality, integrations, maintenance and general WordPress development. I'm currently looking for a long-term remote agency partnership and can work around 20–40 hours/week. My portfolio: https://webleta.com/ If you ever need an extra WordPress developer to take work off your team's plate, I'd be happy to have a quick chat. Thanks, Graham

Personalize one sentence based on something real you saw on their website. Don't lead with your rate.

LinkedIn version

Hi [Name], I came across [Agency Name] and noticed you do quite a bit of WordPress work. I'm a senior WordPress developer based in the Philippines with 7+ years of experience, mainly working with Divi, Elementor, WooCommerce, ACF and custom HTML/CSS/JS. I help agencies handle their website production workload and I'm looking for a long-term remote partnership, around 20–40 hours/week. Portfolio: https://webleta.com/ If you ever need another developer on your production team, I'd be happy to chat. Thanks, Graham

Follow-up

Hi [Name], Just wanted to follow up on my previous message. If your team ever needs additional WordPress development capacity for client projects, I'd be happy to help on an ongoing basis. Happy to have a quick chat if it could be useful. Thanks, Graham

Timing: first follow-up after 5–7 business days; second 7–10 days later; final 2–3 weeks later. Then pause.

08

Upwork strategy

Choose long-term opportunities over tiny one-off tasks.

Prioritize

  • Ongoing WordPress development
  • 20–40 hours/week
  • Divi/Elementor across client sites
  • Agency production support
  • Maintenance and recurring improvements

Usually skip

  • Unrealistically low budgets for large builds
  • Vague scope and unclear expectations
  • Uncredible client history
  • Work unrelated to your target positioning

Proposal opening

Hi, I'm a senior WordPress developer with 7+ years of experience, specializing in Divi, Elementor, WooCommerce and custom WordPress development. I've worked extensively with Figma-to-WordPress builds, responsive implementation, custom CSS/JS, ACF, integrations, performance optimization and ongoing website maintenance. I can work as an ongoing extension of your development team rather than just handling a single project. Portfolio: https://webleta.com/ [Add 2–3 sentences directly addressing the client's project.]
